# D10 Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: September 7, 2026**

D10 is designed to help you make better daily decisions using information available on your device. Your privacy is a core part of that design.

## Information D10 Uses

With your permission, D10 may access information from:

- Apple Health, such as sleep, activity, workouts, heart rate, resting heart rate, and heart rate variability.
- Your calendar, such as events, schedules, and available time.
- Information you enter directly into D10, including your standards, commitments, daily responses, and other information you choose to provide.

## How Your Information Is Used

D10 uses this information to provide its features, including understanding your day, generating daily guidance, helping you make decisions, and maintaining your experience over time.

## Data Collection and Sharing

The current version of D10 processes and stores your personal D10 information on your device.

D10 does not sell your personal information.

D10 does not use your Health data for advertising, marketing, or data brokerage.

D10 does not send your Health data, calendar information, standards, commitments, or other personal D10 information to D10-operated servers in the current version of the app.

## Apple Health

Access to Apple Health is optional and requires your permission. D10 requests read access only to Health data used by the app.

D10 does not write data to Apple Health.

You can change D10's access to Health data through your iPhone's privacy and Health settings.

## Calendar

Calendar access is optional and requires your permission. D10 uses calendar information to understand the structure and demands of your day.

You can change D10's calendar access through your iPhone's privacy settings.

## Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. D10 does not receive or store your full payment card information.

## Children's Privacy

D10 is not directed to children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as D10 evolves. If D10's data practices materially change, this policy will be updated to reflect those changes.
